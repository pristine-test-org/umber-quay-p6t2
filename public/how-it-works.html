How Umber Quay works

Umber Quay keeps one shared list for your whole team. Anyone can add an item, tick it off, and see the change on the landing page within seconds.

Three steps Simple

  • Add an item to the shared list.
  • Tick it off once it is done.
  • Watch the live list on the landing page update.

The list shows the five most recent items, newest first.
